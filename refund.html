Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Because Arqiteque builds custom, bespoke digital architecture, our refund policy is strictly defined to protect the time and intellectual labor invested in your project.

1. The 50% Downpayment

The initial 50% downpayment is strictly non-refundable once the project scope is agreed upon and development has commenced. This downpayment compensates Arqiteque for reserved time in our development queue, initial architecture planning, and design labor already performed.

2. Cancellations by the Client

If the client chooses to cancel the project after development has begun, the 50% downpayment is forfeited. If the project is cancelled after the staging site is presented but before the final 50% payment, the client will not receive the source code or final assets.

3. Cancellations by Arqiteque

If Arqiteque is unable to complete the project for any reason, the client will be refunded a prorated amount based on the percentage of work not yet completed.

4. Final Deliverables

Once the final 50% payment is made and the source code is handed over, the transaction is final. Refunds cannot be issued after the code ownership has been transferred to the client.

5. Digital Products

If Arqiteque sells digital products (such as PDFs or templates), all sales are final. No refunds will be issued once the secure download link has been generated and accessed.
