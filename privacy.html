Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At ARQITEQUE, privacy is engineered into our architecture. Because our compiler and agent runtime execute client-side, your data footprint remains minimal.

1. Information We Process

We adhere to strict zero-bloat data principles:

  • Direct Communications: If you contact us via email or direct message, we retain your contact handle solely to respond to your inquiry.
  • Local Compilation Payload: Code tested within our live compiler sandbox runs locally in your browser and is not retained on our servers.
  • Agentic Requests: When an external agent or user calls our agent-manifest.json or invokes the Necessity Engine over the network, the HTML payload is processed in memory and discarded. We do not log or store the content of agent requests.
  • Infrastructure Telemetry: Standard hosting metrics (such as anonymous page views and performance analytics via Vercel) are collected to ensure platform stability.

2. How Data is Utilized

Any collected information is used strictly for maintaining platform performance, responding to developer inquiries, and optimizing our agentic network interfaces.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure

ARQITEQUE is hosted via secure cloud infrastructure (Vercel and GitHub). These providers operate under their own independent privacy and security standards.

4. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries or data removal requests, reach out directly to arqiteque.build@gmail.com.
