Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At Arqiteque, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy outlines how we handle the information you provide to us.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to build and deliver your project. This includes:

  • Contact Information: Your name, email address, and phone number when you initiate contact via our website or Facebook page.
  • Project Data: Any text, images, or business details you provide for the website build.
  • Payment Data: We do not store your credit card or payment details on our servers. All payments are processed securely via third-party gateways (such as GCash, Maya, or bank transfers).

2. How We Use Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is used strictly for:

  • Communicating with you about your project.
  • Building and deploying your website.
  • Sending invoices and processing payments.

3. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party tools (like Vercel for hosting, GitHub for code management, and external payment gateways). These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

4. Data Retention

We retain your contact information for as long as necessary to maintain your website or for future maintenance requests. If you wish to have your data deleted from our records, please contact us.
